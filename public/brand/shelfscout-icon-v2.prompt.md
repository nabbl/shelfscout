# ShelfScout icon v2

Created using the built-in image generation tool. Standalone app icon concept following feedback that the bookshelf wordmark was too generic.

## Initial prompt

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: standalone ShelfScout app icon and favicon concept.
Primary request: Create a distinctive symbol for ShelfScout, a private book-discovery companion. The previous logo of three books on a shelf was rejected as too generic. This new design must be identifiable at favicon size and must have personality through its silhouette.
Design direction: A single bold custom capital S monogram constructed from TWO chunky interlocking folded-book shapes. The upper and lower forms are related by a half turn, together clearly reading as an S. Hint at a book cover and one folded page using the contour and only one broad negative-space cut in each form. Treat this like a masterful mid-century publisher's emblem: sculptural flat geometry, slightly unexpected angled terminals, excellent optical balance, instantly memorable. Prioritize the clear S silhouette over literal book illustration. Minimal shapes; broad open counters and thick strokes; no fine lines. The symbol must survive at 16 by 16 pixels.
Composition: One standalone cream symbol centered on a solid square forest green field #245a49; symbol occupies about 72 percent of width and height, generous consistent safe margin. Square 1024 by 1024 artwork, full bleed flat background. No rounded outer corners, so platforms can apply their own masks.
Palette: two flat colors only, warm cream #f8f7f2 and forest green #245a49.
Style: precise vector-like graphic design, flat solid fills, quiet literary confidence. Human-designed custom identity with a distinctive outline, not a stock library icon.
Text: no wordmark, no letters apart from the S embodied by the symbol.
Avoid: rows of books, bookshelf pictograms, open-book clipart, generic rounded S font glyph, tiny book spine stripes, ornate detail, gradients, texture, shading, shadows, 3D, sparkles, stars, magnifying glass, compass, robot, eyes, owl, mockup, multiple options, presentation board, captions, watermarks.

## Refinement prompt

Edit this ShelfScout icon into clean final flat artwork. Preserve the overall distinctive book-shaped S silhouette and the orientation of the two folded terminals. Simplify each terminal to ONE wide triangular negative-space notch, remove all thin page stripes. Remove ALL speckles, grunge, edge noise, distress, shading and texture. Render the S as one perfectly uniform solid warm cream #f8f7f2 with crisp smooth edges. Crucial: fill the ENTIRE square canvas and ALL negative-space areas with uniform opaque forest green #245a49, alpha 255 throughout. This is an app icon with a solid colored background, NOT a transparent cutout. Do not remove the green background. Keep the mark centered with 15% clear margin on every side. Broad open counters that remain visible at 16px. Two flat colors only. No additional objects, no text, no mockup.
