## Final prompt

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: final logo artwork for ShelfScout, a private self-hosted book-discovery companion that learns a reader's taste and helps find their next book.
Primary request: Design one distinctive, understated, professionally art-directed logo that feels made by a human graphic designer for an independent literary publisher. The user explicitly wants it not to look AI-generated.
Design: a compact custom solid-ink symbol of three book spines on a short shelf, one book leaning outward as if just discovered. Reduce this to a few strong, beautifully balanced shapes; subtle asymmetry, generous negative space, recognisable at small size. Pair the symbol to the left of the exact wordmark "ShelfScout" in a characterful restrained literary serif, akin to Instrument Serif but with careful optical kerning and a confident readable weight. The symbol and lettering should feel like one considered identity.
Palette: single flat forest green #245a49, from the actual application's palette.
Background: genuinely transparent background, no paper texture.
Composition: one horizontal logo lockup centered, generous clear space but logo fills about 80% of canvas width; landscape format. Symbol approximately the height of the capital letters or slightly taller.
Text (verbatim): "ShelfScout". Capital S in Shelf and Scout. No other text.
Style: crisp flat vector-like edges, elegant bookish identity, economical forms, quiet personality, timeless printer's mark. Finished production artwork, not a sketch or a presentation board.
Avoid: AI sparkles, stars, gradients, glows, robot motifs, neural networks, orbit rings, magnifying glasses, compass roses, generic tech startup swooshes, mascots, faux distressing, hairline details, 3D, shadows, mockups, extra variations, taglines, watermarks.
